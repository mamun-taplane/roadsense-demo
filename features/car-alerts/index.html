Feature design for FRD "CarPlay & Android Auto Alerts" (Nov 11, 2026) · 800×480 car screens · light and dark · driver mode

RequirementApple CarPlayAndroid Auto
One blocker for Android Auto. Android Auto only runs apps in the categories it lists: media, messaging, calling, navigation, points of interest, IoT and weather (supported app categories). An alert-only app fits none of them. The only way in is the navigation category, and a navigation app must give turn-by-turn directions (NF-1).
