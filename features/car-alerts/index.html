CarPlay & Android Auto Alerts

The active alert's icon and message on the car screen, read aloud, next to the driver's navigation app or as the main screen. No map. Every screen is drawn as the template CarPlay or Android Auto really gives a non-map app.

Feature design for FRD "CarPlay & Android Auto Alerts" (Nov 11, 2026) · 800×480 car screens · light and dark · driver mode

RequirementApple CarPlayAndroid Auto
One blocker for Android Auto. Android Auto only runs apps in the categories it lists: media, messaging, calling, navigation, points of interest, IoT and weather (supported app categories). An alert-only app fits none of them. The only way in is the navigation category, and a navigation app must give turn-by-turn directions (NF-1). The Android Auto screens below show the design if Road Sense ships as a navigation app. Decision for the team.

REQ-001

Minimal alert screen

Only the active alert's icon and message. No map, no navigation. When nothing is happening, a quiet "Road Sense is running" screen. The alert changes the moment a new one starts; it doesn't count down.

REQ-002

Next to the navigation app

Spotify gets a panel next to navigation because it is a media app. Road Sense isn't, so each platform gives it a different place. Road Sense as the main screen is REQ-001.

REQ-003

Audio alerts in the car

The phone speaks each alert once, through the car speakers, whatever is on the car screen. Music and the navigation app's voice duck under it. Tap a line to hear it.

REQ-004

In-vehicle alert settings

Two switches on the phone: audio and visual. The car screens show what each switch does.

REQ-005

CarPlay and Android Auto parity

Same alerts, same words, same icons, same settings on both. What differs is the template each platform gives.

FeatureApple CarPlayAndroid Auto
REQ-006

Assets and configuration prefetch

On first launch the app downloads every alert icon plus the global and Connected Utah settings, then keeps them current in the background. The car screens only take images that are already on the phone, so this is also a platform need.

For the team to decide

1. Android Auto category. Ship Road Sense as a navigation app (turn-by-turn needed, NF-1), or leave Android Auto at voice alerts from the phone only?
2. iOS 26 for the CarPlay panel. The Live Activity next to navigation needs iOS 26 (CarPlay guide p. 11). Older iPhones: notification + voice only.
3. Android Auto voice while Google Maps guides. A second navigation app must not give voice guidance then (NF-5), and the navigation audio channel is for navigation instructions only (DD-1). Ask Google whether safety alerts count.
4. CarPlay notification permission. Ask for it with the other launch permissions (p. 28).
